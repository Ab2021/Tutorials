## ⚡ The 3 Things the Panel Is Explicitly Testing

Based on the panel feedback, these are the **actual gap areas** that caused rejections:

1. **Product Development Mindset** — Can you think beyond the model to the full product lifecycle?
2. **Service Design + Stakeholder Integration** — Can you work across UK/India teams, explain AI to non-technical stakeholders?
3. **Technical + People Mentoring** — Have you grown engineers? Do you have a mentoring philosophy?

## 🎤 The 8 Questions That Were Asked (Previous Candidate)

From the candidate feedback, these exact questions were asked:

1. Which components of Azure AI you worked on?
2. Explain RAG architecture in detail with code?
3. How big of a team you worked on?
4. How did you evaluate your agents?
5. How can you have linear commit history in Git, when working with multiple people in a team?
6. Draw a system design diagram — images → model → anomaly detection?
7. RAG code review: explain and suggest improvements?
8. Deep-dive into your latest project with major questions about it.

**All 8 are covered in depth in this preparation set.**
