## Why System Design Matters in 2026

System design is now one of the most important rounds across AI interviews. A year ago, "design a system that serves an LLM" was reserved for ML engineer roles — now it shows up in general software engineer loops too.

**Three reasons it's critical:**

1. **It decides your salary and level** — not just pass/fail.
2. **60%+ of ML technical interviews now include GenAI questions** — LLM behavior, hallucination mitigation, prompt engineering.
3. **You must explain your thinking out loud** — silence is now a red flag.
