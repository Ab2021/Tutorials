## 1. Module purpose

This module teaches resource-based supply: reserves and grade, cut-off and blending, extraction and recovery, pit-to-port logistics, commodity offtake, forestry harvest, and social and environmental licence.

The survey file gives the module's narrative arc in one pass. This folder breaks that arc into
teaching units. Each topic file is a self-contained 2-4 hour block with mechanics, a worked
example, code or calculation, failure modes, assumptions, and exercises.

Read `_index.md` first, then the topics in order. Topics assume the prerequisites listed below,
not the whole program.

## 4. How the topics fit together

Each topic is written to stand alone, but they are ordered so that later topics can assume the
vocabulary of earlier ones. If you are short on time, the minimum path is the first topic plus
any topic that names your current decision problem.

```text
01 Reserves, Grade, and Confidence ->
02 Mine Planning and Cut-Off Grade ->
03 Drilling, Blasting, and Load-Haul ->
04 Grade Blending and Specification ->
05 Processing and Recovery ->
06 Pit-to-Port Logistics ->
07 Stockpiles and Bulk Inventory ->
08 Commodity Price and Offtake ->
09 Forestry Harvest and Chain of Custody ->
10 Social Licence, Environment, and Closure
```

## 5. What this module does not cover

- It does not cover geology, geostatistics, or mining engineering design.
- It does not cover metallurgy or detailed processing plant design.
- Hands-on lab execution: see the lab sections of the survey file.
- Legal advice: resource reporting, permits, and land rights require professional review.
