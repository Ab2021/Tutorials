## 1. Module purpose

This module teaches international physical flow: Incoterms, landed cost, classification and origin, port operations, demurrage, freight procurement, chokepoints, and trade policy.

The survey file gives the module's narrative arc in one pass. This folder breaks that arc into
teaching units. Each topic file is a self-contained 2-4 hour block with mechanics, a worked
example, code or calculation, failure modes, assumptions, and exercises.

Read `_index.md` first, then the topics in order. Topics assume the prerequisites listed below,
not the whole program.

## 4. How the topics fit together

Each topic is written to stand alone, but they are ordered so that later topics can assume the
vocabulary of earlier ones. If you are short on time, the minimum path is the first topic plus
any topic that names your current decision problem.

```text
01 Incoterms and Trade Documents ->
02 Total Landed Cost ->
03 Classification, Valuation, and Origin ->
04 Modal Choice and Emissions ->
05 Port Operations and Congestion ->
06 Demurrage and Detention ->
07 Freight Procurement and Rate Risk ->
08 Chokepoints and Network Resilience ->
09 Trade Policy, Sanctions, and Carbon Rules ->
10 Customs and Broker Management
```

## 5. What this module does not cover

- It does not cover maritime engineering, vessel design, or navigation.
- It does not provide legal or customs advice; classification and origin are professional judgements.
- Hands-on lab execution: see the lab sections of the survey file.
- Commercial freight index data must be licensed; verify terms before use.
