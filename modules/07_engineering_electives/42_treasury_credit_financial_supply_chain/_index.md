## 1. Module purpose

This module teaches the financial layer of the supply chain: cash conversion, liquidity forecasting, counterparty credit, trade finance, FX and interest-rate hedging, and the governance of supply chain finance.

The survey file gives the module's narrative arc in one pass. This folder breaks that arc into
teaching units. Each topic file is a self-contained 2-4 hour block with mechanics, a worked
example, code or calculation, failure modes, assumptions, and exercises.

Read `_index.md` first, then the topics in order. Topics assume the prerequisites listed below,
not the whole program.

## 4. How the topics fit together

Each topic is written to stand alone, but they are ordered so that later topics can assume the
vocabulary of earlier ones. If you are short on time, the minimum path is the first topic plus
any topic that names your current decision problem.

```text
01 Cash Conversion Cycle ->
02 Cash-Flow Forecasting ->
03 Counterparty Credit Risk ->
04 Trade Finance Instruments ->
05 Reverse Factoring and Dynamic Discounting ->
06 FX and Interest-Rate Hedging ->
07 NPV and Cost of Capital ->
08 Credit Policy and Limits ->
09 KYC, AML, Sanctions, and Fraud ->
10 Supply Chain Finance Disclosure and Ethics
```

## 5. What this module does not cover

- It does not cover corporate finance theory, equity valuation, or capital markets in depth.
- It does not cover tax structuring or transfer pricing implementation: those need specialist advice.
- Hands-on lab execution: see the lab sections of the survey file.
- Legal advice: financial regulation and sanctions content requires professional review.
