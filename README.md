## What it deliberately does not contain

Topic files are indexed at 1,200–1,800 words each but not yet written; the survey files are
complete at survey depth and can be taught on their own. Out-of-scope boundaries — customs
brokerage, legal and tax advice, clinical medicine, classified defence, commodity trading
strategy, vendor internals — are stated in `course/TOPIC_COVERAGE_MAP.md` §13.

## Conventions

- **Evidence grading:** A peer-reviewed or audited; B regulatory or official statistic; C company
  case or investor claim; D vendor marketing; E press summary or anecdote. Every case names its grade.
- **No fabricated sources.** Citations point to issuing bodies and named works; no invented DOIs or URLs.
- **Vendor claims are labelled.** Product capabilities are not presented as independent findings.
- **Module numbers are stable.** 00–30 are the core; 01A and 14A are insertions that were never
  renumbered; 31–45 are electives.
