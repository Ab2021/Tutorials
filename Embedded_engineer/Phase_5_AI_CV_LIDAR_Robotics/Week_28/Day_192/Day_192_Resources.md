# Day 192: Bio-Inspired Robotics - External Resources

## 🔬 Practical Labs & GitHub Repositories
1.  **snu-mbe/sorotoki** (Soft Robotics Toolkit)
    *   **Description:** A toolkit for design, modeling, and control of soft robots (MATLAB/Python).
    *   **Link:** [https://github.com/snu-mbe/sorotoki](https://github.com/snu-mbe/sorotoki)
    *   **Relevance:** Excellent for simulating bio-inspired soft structures.

2.  **Bio-inspired Robotics Testbed (BRT)**
    *   **Description:** Framework for simulating evolved soft robots in MuJoCo.
    *   **Link:** [GitHub Search "Bio-inspired Robotics Testbed"](https://github.com/topics/bio-inspired-robotics)
    *   **Relevance:** Combining evolution strategies with physics simulation.
