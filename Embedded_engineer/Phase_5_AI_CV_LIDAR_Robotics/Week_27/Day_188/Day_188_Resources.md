# Day 188: Legged Robot Dynamics - External Resources

## 🔬 Practical Labs & GitHub Repositories
1.  **leggedrobotics/legged_gym**
    *   **Description:** Isaac Gym environments for training legged locomotion policies.
    *   **Link:** [https://github.com/leggedrobotics/legged_gym](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/legged_gym)
    *   **Relevance:** The codebase behind many ETH Zurich breakthroughs in legged learning.

2.  **qiayuanl/legged_control**
    *   **Description:** An OCS2 (Optimal Control for Switched Systems) and ROS 2 Control implementation.
    *   **Link:** [https://github.com/qiayuanl/legged_control](https://github.com/qiayuanl/legged_control)
    *   **Relevance:** High-performance C++ control stack for Unitree robots.

3.  **unitreerobotics/unitree_ros**
    *   **Description:** Official ROS packages for Unitree Go1/A1 robots.
    *   **Link:** [https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros)
