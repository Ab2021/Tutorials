## 📖 The Future of Platform Engineering

### 1. LLMOps -> AgentOps
*   **Today:** "How do I deploy a Llama-2 endpoint?"
*   **Tomorrow:** "How do I orchestrate a swarm of 50 Agents (Planner, Coder, Reviewer) collaborating to write software?"
*   **Challenge:** Debugging non-deterministic agent loops. Tracing "thought processes" (Chain of Thought).

### 2. Green AI (FinOps++)
*   **Today:** "Scale up to 1000 GPUs."
*   **Tomorrow:** "Scale down to zero to save Carbon."
*   **Challenge:** Scheduling workloads based on Grid Carbon Intensity (run in Iceland, not Virginia).

### 3. Edge AI (Federated Learning)
*   **Today:** Send data to Cloud, train, send model back.
*   **Tomorrow:** Train *on* the phone. Aggregated updates sent to cloud (Privacy Preserving).
*   **Challenge:** 1 Billion devices. Heterogeneous hardware.

## 💼 Career Advice: The Staff Engineer

### The "T-Shaped" Engineer
*   **Broad:** You know a little about Frontend, Mobile, Hardware.
*   **Deep:** You are the world expert in *Platform Engineering*.

### Impact > Activity
*   **Junior:** "I wrote 500 lines of code."
*   **Senior:** "I deleted 5000 lines of legacy code."
*   **Staff:** "I convinced the team *not* to build this feature, saving 6 months."

### Glue Work
*   Writing the RFC.
*   Fixing the CI pipeline that everyone complains about.
*   Mentoring the intern.
*   **This is what gets you promoted.**
