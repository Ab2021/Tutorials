## Purpose

This collection will hold cross-module cases for **Data, Analytics and Decision Science**. Unlike module-specific cases, each case should require at least two modules from this part and show business context, constraints, architecture, metrics, trade-offs, failure modes, and evidence.

## Planned case-study formats

1. **Design case:** ambiguous requirements to a justified architecture.
2. **Implementation case:** data/model/platform choices with validation steps.
3. **Incident case:** symptom, blast radius, diagnosis, remediation, and prevention.
4. **Decision case:** compare alternatives using cost, risk, quality, latency, and adoption criteria.

## Candidate threads

- Data Modelling, Databases, Lakehouse and Governance: Model an e-commerce lakehouse; repair a broken customer 360 pipeline
- Analytics, Metrics, BI and Product Sense: Diagnose marketplace conversion decline; create an executive metric tree
- Experimentation, Causal Inference and Decision Science: Evaluate a pricing change; measure an outreach intervention
- Data Streaming, Event Systems and Real-Time Analytics: Build fraud-alert streaming design; debug consumer lag incident
- Time-Series Forecasting and Uncertainty: Forecast spare-parts demand; plan cloud capacity with prediction intervals
