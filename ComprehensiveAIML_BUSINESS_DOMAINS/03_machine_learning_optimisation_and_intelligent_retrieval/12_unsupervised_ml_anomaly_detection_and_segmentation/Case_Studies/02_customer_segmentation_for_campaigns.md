> **Evidence:** illustrative teaching design; no company segmentation, campaign, or measured outcome is asserted.

## 1. The One-Line Framing
Given eligible customer records at a dated campaign cut, select segments and offers for the lifecycle-marketing owner to improve incremental contribution subject to contact consent, frequency caps, and a fixed campaign capacity, compared with one undifferentiated offer.
**Takeaway:** the segment is decision-ready only within a stated population, time boundary, capacity, and evidence limit.
