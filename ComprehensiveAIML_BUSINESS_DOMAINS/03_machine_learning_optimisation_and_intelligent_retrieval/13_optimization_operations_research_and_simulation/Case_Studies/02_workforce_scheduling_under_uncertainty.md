## 6. Analysis Walkthrough
```text
FORMULA [STAFF-01]: shortage = max(0, demand - staffed)
WHERE: demand and staffed are qualified worker-hours in one interval.
CONSTRAINT: skills and rest constraints must already be feasible.
NUMERICAL EXAMPLE: demand=18 hours, staffed=15 hours, shortage=3 hours.
INTERPRETATION: a low-cost roster with shortage is not equivalent to a feasible service plan.
```
Sensitivity: if demand is 14, shortage is zero with one hour slack; if demand is 20, shortage is five. The formula does not price customer harm without a separately stated cost model.

**Recovery:** withdraw the affected assignment and use the approved contingency process; do not relabel an exception as baseline feasibility.
**Verification:** independent coverage calculation agrees with the schedule and shows the same shortage or slack.
**Boundary:** a feasible roster is not proof of employee acceptance, service quality, or realized demand.
