# Data Science Method Playbooks

> **Purpose:** Reference structures for the major DS disciplines recorded in the audit.

## Domain folders

| # | Domain / capability | Scope | Indexed topics |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 01 | [Computer Vision Playbook](./01_computer_vision_playbook/INDEX.md) | Full audit-derived CV sequence from image fundamentals to multimodal, 3D, video, deployment, and governance. | 19 |
| 02 | [Fraud Modelling Playbook](./02_fraud_modelling_playbook/INDEX.md) | A reference-led companion to the production fraud case family with probability, sampling, detection, investigation, and operations coverage. | 16 |
| 03 | [KDA Pharma Playbook](./03_kda_pharma_playbook/INDEX.md) | A structured reference for the audit KDA topics, from foundations through technology, cases, interview practice, and applications. | 18 |

| 06 | [Pricing Playbook](./06_pricing_playbook/INDEX.md) | The audit pricing sequence from economics and elasticity through auctions, fairness, experimentation, and real-time systems. | 20 |

## Collection usage

This family has its own cross-domain Case_Studies and Cheat_Sheets folders. Use these for comparisons that span its child domains; use each child collection for domain-specific material.
