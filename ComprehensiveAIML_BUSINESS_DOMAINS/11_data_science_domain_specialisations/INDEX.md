## Company-name policy

Company names in indexes are learning contexts, comparative-research targets, or public-case candidates. They do not assert a company uses a particular method. A drafted case must label its evidence as observed, published, estimated, simulated, illustrative, or not-measured, and cite evidence for any operational claim.

## Case-study standard

These are planned catalogs, not completed cases or certified template-compliant collections. All future content follows the [course authoring and numerical standards](../00_AUTHORING_AND_NUMERICAL_STANDARDS.md), including the current case master's 15-section individual outline and seven-section collection INDEX, and the cheat-sheet master's 12-section individual outline and 11-section collection INDEX. Each topic needs its own derivations/mechanisms, checked numerical examples, failure diagnostics and answered interview pack before publication.
